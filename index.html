Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and processes.

How long does the business name registration process take?
The business name registration process typically takes 3-5 business days once all required documents are submitted. We expedite the process by ensuring your application is complete and accurate before submission.
What documents are required for trademark registration?
For trademark registration, you'll need a clear representation of your trademark, details of the applicant (individual or company), a list of goods/services the trademark will cover, and proof of payment for the application fee.
What is the difference between the Diamond Mark and Standardization Mark?
The Diamond Mark is a quality certification mark issued by KEBS for locally manufactured products that meet Kenyan standards. The Standardization Mark is for products that comply with Kenyan standards but may be manufactured locally or imported.
Do you provide support for international businesses seeking certification in Kenya?
Yes, we assist both local and international businesses with certification and compliance processes in Kenya. We can help navigate the specific requirements for imported products and facilitate communication with relevant Kenyan authorities.
